New rules by 1 January 2027

Every Queensland home needs interconnected photoelectric smoke alarms.

In every bedroom, the hallways that connect them, and on every storey. Older ionisation alarms and removable-battery units won't meet the standard. Not sure if your place complies? Jarid can check what you've got, supply what's missing and issue your compliance certificate.
